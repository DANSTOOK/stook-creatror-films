Fonts bundled for titles
========================

Three families, each under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, shipped exactly as
their authors publish them: no subsetting, no conversion, no renaming inside
the files (only the file names below differ). Source Serif carries the
Reserved Font Name "Source", which is why it must stay unmodified. Their
licences are shown in the app under Help > About > Font licences.

Downloaded on 2026-09-27 from the official repositories only.
